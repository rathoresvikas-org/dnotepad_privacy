# Privacy Policy for DNotepad

**Effective Date:** August 23, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 23, 2026

**DNotepad** is an offline text and source code editor developed by RathoreSVikas. We are committed to complete privacy and transparency. DNotepad is engineered from the ground up as a **100% offline application**. 

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Internet Transmission

* **No Internet Connectivity:** DNotepad operates completely offline. The application does not connect to the internet, does not use external servers, and does not require an account or login.
* **No Telemetry or Tracking:** DNotepad contains no analytics tools, no crash reporting SDKs, and no advertising networks. We do not track or log what you type, read, or edit.
* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, harvest, buy, sell, or share personal information (such as your name, email address, IP address, device identifiers, or location).

---

### 2. How Your Files and Documents Are Handled

* **100% On-Device Processing:** All text documents, code files, and scripts that you open, create, or edit in DNotepad are processed and stored exclusively on your device's local storage.
* **No Cloud Sync:** DNotepad does not upload or synchronize your documents to any cloud service or third-party server. Your files never leave your device.
* **File Ownership:** You maintain exclusive ownership and control over all files created or modified with DNotepad.

---

### 3. Device Permissions

DNotepad requests only the permissions necessary to function as an offline file and source code editor:

* **Storage Access (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):**
  * *Purpose:* Required solely to enable the core functionality of the application—allowing you to browse, open, view, edit, and save text and source code files across your device's internal storage and external SD cards.

---

### 4. Data Control and Deletion

Because all files reside strictly on your device's storage:
* You can modify or delete your files at any time using DNotepad or any file manager of your choice.
* Uninstalling DNotepad or clearing the app's cache in Android Settings will remove all application settings and temporary caches without altering your saved documents on external storage.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

DNotepad is an offline text editor that does not collect any personal data from any user, including children under the age of 13. The app is completely safe and private for users of all ages.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we introduce new features or updates to DNotepad in future releases, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly to reflect those changes.
